FAQ

Branding questions

What do I get in a branding project?

You get a main logo with an icon and colour versions, a colour palette and fonts that fit your business, and a brand guide so everything you post matches. We can also design print items like business cards, flyers, signboards and packaging.

How does the branding process work?

We start by learning your story, customers and goals. Then we share logo ideas and a mood board, refine the design with your feedback, and deliver all logo files together with your brand guide.

Can you create brand guidelines for my team?

Yes. We create a clear guide that explains how to use your logo, colours, fonts and design rules, so staff, designers and printers keep every post and print item on-brand, like the Emma Thinking guide on this page.
