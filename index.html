FAQ

Questions

Ask us anything on WhatsApp.

What is Zylara.co?

Zylara.co is a business growth partner in Sri Lanka. We help businesses grow with branding, social media, photo and video, Meta and Google ads, business development, websites and apps, and CRM systems, all from one team.

Are you only a marketing agency?

No. Marketing is only one part of what we do. We also build brands, plan business growth, make websites and apps, and set up CRM systems, so one team looks after your whole growth plan instead of several different suppliers.

How much does it cost?

Every business is different, so we don't sell fixed packages. We start with a free audit, then send a clear quote based on your goals and the work you need. If you run ads, the ad budget is paid directly to Meta or Google.

How do we start working together?

Message us on WhatsApp or send the form on this page. We learn about your story, customers and goals, then share a growth plan and content calendar for your confirmation before any work goes live.

Do I approve work before it goes live?

Always. Every post, ad, design and page is sent to you first, and nothing goes live without your confirmation. After launch, you get a clear report every month showing what worked and what comes next.

Do you run Meta and Google ads?

Yes. We plan, build and run ads on Facebook, Instagram and Google, track which ads bring enquiries, and check results every week. We also have regular calls with Meta's team to stay up to date with new ad features.

Can you build a CRM for my business?

Yes. We build a CRM around how your team already handles leads and customers, with WhatsApp follow-ups, reminders, staff dashboards and sales reports for owners. We also train your team so they can use it from day one.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

We work with many kinds of businesses, including education, tourism, retail, food export and vehicle platforms. Our clients are in Sri Lanka and overseas, like Nexora in the UK.
