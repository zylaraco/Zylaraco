FAQ

Photo and video questions

What photo and video services do you offer?

We shoot product and place photos, reels and TikToks, video ads and event coverage. Every file is sized for where it will be used, from 9:16 reels and 4:5 feed posts to YouTube videos and website banners.

Can you plan our event as well as film it?

Yes. We help with the event idea and theme, venue, decor and setup, music, games and entertainment, and guest invites and promotion. On the day, our team captures the photos and video.

Where do you shoot?

At your shop, office or any location you choose. We plan the shoot with ideas, a script and a shot list, then edit with colour, music, captions and cuts, and deliver files ready to post.
